## Weekly practice template

For several hours of practice per week:

| Block | Time | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Ear and theory | 20 min, 3 times a week | Interval and chord ear training, spelling drills, the quizzes |
| Vocabulary | 20 min, 3 times a week | The current module's scales and chords on guitar and piano |
| Improvisation | 15 min, 3 times a week | Chord-tone soloing over loops, rotating keys |
| Writing | 45–60 min, twice a week | The current module's writing exercises, or the weekly song |
| Listening and analysis | 20 min, once a week | Analyze one song with the checklist below |

If you have less time, keep the writing blocks and shorten the others. Writing is the point of the course.

## The daily minimum

On days when you have only ten minutes:

1. Three minutes of ear training.
2. Five minutes on one exercise from the current lesson.
3. Two minutes recording one new idea, however small.

## Analysis checklist

Use this on any song. Twenty minutes with one song teaches more than two hours of passive listening.

1. **Key and mode.** What is home? Major, minor or modal?
2. **Chords as numerals.** Write out the progression of each section.
3. **Outside chords.** Any borrowed chords or secondary dominants? Where, and why there?
4. **Form.** Map the sections with bar counts. Draw the energy graph.
5. **Melody.** What is the motif? How is it varied? Which chord tones are targeted? Where is the climax?
6. **Rhythm.** What is the subdivision? Where is the syncopation? What does the kick drum do?
7. **Arrangement.** What enters and exits at each section boundary?
8. **Steal one move.** Pick one thing and use it in your own writing this week.
