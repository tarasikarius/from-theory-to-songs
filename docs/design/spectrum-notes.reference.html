Power chords

The idea

A power chord is two notes: a root and the perfect fifth above it, seven semitones up. A5 is A and E. With no third, it is neither major nor minor, which is why it stays clear under heavy distortion.

A5 on the fretboard

A|--7--|
E|--5--|
